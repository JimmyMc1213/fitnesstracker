# Launch outreach kit (strangers, not F&F)

Use **only after** TestFlight works and **you** completed pay → unblur on your phone.

Parker's rule: friends/family are too nice. Measure strangers at the paywall.

---

## One asset

Screen-record on iPhone (60–90 sec):

1. Open app → quick onboarding montage
2. Upload selfie
3. Blurred Future You on paywall (tease — don't spoil unblur in the hook clip)
4. Optional: separate "reveal" clip for paid ads later

Save to Photos. This feeds all channels.

---

## Post captions (pick one)

**TikTok / Reels**

> I built an app that shows you what you could look like if you actually stuck to the plan. Upload a photo → see your Future You. Link in bio.

**X**

> Shipped something I've been building for months — AI Future You + full fitness coach in one app. Strangers only: trying to see if the paywall hook works. [TestFlight or App Store link]

## Lightweight outreach (not F&F)

- [ ] Post 2–3 public clips (don't send to family group chat first)
- [ ] DM 5–10 fitness creators or gym acquaintances (**not** close friends)
- [ ] Optional: one post in a fitness subreddit — follow rules, no spam

**Goal:** 20–30 strangers complete onboarding and hit the paywall.

**Measure:** pay vs bounce at blur. Ask every payer: *What almost made you not pay?*

## Distribution (decide when ready)

- Public TestFlight link in bio, or
- App Store listing

Defer until payments are live.
