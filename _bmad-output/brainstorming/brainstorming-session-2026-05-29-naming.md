# Brainstorming Session Results — Product Naming

**Facilitator:** Jimmymccarthy  
**Date:** 2026-05-29  
**Product context:** All-in-one fitness PWA — workouts, nutrition, habits, progress, rule-based coaching. Not Gymmy. Fitcoach is placeholder only.

## Session Overview

**Topic:** Final consumer-facing app name  
**Goals:** High-volume divergent names; avoid Fit-/Gym-/Coach- clichés and major competitor collisions (Strong, Hevy, Fitbod, Strava, MyFitnessPal, Nike Training, Peloton, etc.)

### Constraints (session rules)

- Prefer **coined, short, pronounceable** names (4–8 chars ideal for icon label)
- Avoid **-ly** startup suffixes and obvious **Fit/Gym/Coach** compounds
- Favor names that evoke **guidance + consistency** (streaks, weekly rhythm, check-ins) without claiming "AI coach"
- **Availability not verified** — manual App Store, domain, USPTO pass required on shortlist

**Approach:** AI-Recommended Techniques
